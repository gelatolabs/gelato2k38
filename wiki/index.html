History

Gelato System 2K38 originated in the deepest recesses of two mentally unstable Gelato Labs systems engineers - Krypto Kyle and Matthew Moody. One fine sunny day while Kyle & Matthew were recovering from an extended session of heavy drinking (vodka martinis - shaken, not stirred) and programming they came to the conclusion that there was a need for a different type of OS - one built by developers - for developers. It wasn't a simple task by any means, but after 8 years, 23 days, 19 minutes, and 2 seconds their dream became a reality: Gelato System 2K38!
